# Editorial Safety

Black Sheep uses neutral wording, status explanations, and source attribution. Being linked to a record does not imply guilt. Roles are shown per case so witnesses, complainants, beneficiaries, related people, accused people, defendants, and convicted people are not conflated.

Acquittals, dismissed cases, withdrawn charges, overturned convictions, and closed-without-charge records must be displayed as prominently as allegations or charges.

Corrections and subject responses are first-class public records. Private identifiers, precise private addresses, and unverified private financial details are excluded.
